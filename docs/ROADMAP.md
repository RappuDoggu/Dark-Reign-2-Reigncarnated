# Planned features

0.1 supports offline play. The following features are planned; there are no release dates yet.

## Next

- Campaign fixes: objectives, dialogue, transitions and endings for both factions.
- Rendering fixes: fog of war, lighting, textures and effects.
- Save/resume and reliable campaign progress.
- Better zoom limits, camera controls and touch selection.
- Longer-match stability, audio fixes and testing across more Android devices.

## Maps and mods

- Additional Instant Action maps.
- Custom-map import and selection.
- Easier mod import, selection and management.

## Graphics and controls

- Upscaling and render-quality options, with device performance in mind.
- Adjustable zoom and optional zoom controls that work alongside two-finger map pan.
- Configurable controller, keyboard and mouse support.

## Multiplayer

- LAN matches between Android devices.
- Match setup, synchronization, disconnect handling and reconnect testing.
- Online play after LAN support is reliable.

Multiplayer, saves, mod management and the new graphics/control options are not available in 0.1.
Original PC crossplay is not confirmed.
